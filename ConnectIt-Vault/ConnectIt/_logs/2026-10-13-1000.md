## Request

Rework the win condition setup: declare every goal up front as an array of win condition groups; when a group is satisfied a
checkpoint delegate fires, and when all groups are complete the win delegate fires.

## Overview

Each side now lists its win condition groups in order (`PlayerWinGroups` / `AIWinGroups`). The board records how many each
faction has completed (`GroupsCompleted`); `StampWinState` advances them after every move, appending a `WinCheckpointReached` board
event for each group completed that is not the last. `AConnectIt_GameMode` turns that event into the Blueprint delegate
`OnWinCheckpointReached` and broadcasts `OnMatchWon` on the win. The pre-commit `OnWinConditionsMet` hook, `SetWinConditions` and the
re-check loop from 2026-10-08 were removed; `SetLoadout` stays for a Blueprint on the checkpoint delegate.
