## Request

Stage 4 of [first-tile-powers-plan](../design/first-tile-powers-plan.md): tile hooks, reaction handles and the tile
visualizer.

## Overview

- **Reaction handle:** `UConnectIt_TileReaction` has a `Finish` a hook calls once its animation ends. A second
  Finish is ignored.
- **Seven tile hooks on `AConnectIt_TileBase`:** power triggered, piece enter / exit, multiplier changed, lock
  changed, active changed, chain partner changed. Their defaults finish at once. A tile finishes what it still holds
  when it leaves play.
- **`UConnectIt_TileVisualizer`**, created by the board registry subsystem on every machine:
  - registers a persistent gated task for each of 12 board event tags;
  - calls the matching hooks on the tile actors with fresh reactions;
  - completes when every reaction has finished.
  - Completion during its own dispatch waits a tick, because the plugin's queue is mid-loop over that phase's tasks.
- **Build and tests:** built; 62/62 tests pass (new `ConnectIt.Board.TileHooks`). Throughput 361,177 nodes/s,
  unchanged.
