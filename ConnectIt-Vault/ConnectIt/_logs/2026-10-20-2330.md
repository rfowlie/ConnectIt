## Request

Build part 2 of the 2026-10-20 plan, approved by the owner: [design/three-directors-plan](../design/three-directors-plan.md)
(three directors, conditions in directors, lose conditions, level triggers, level changes). This note grows with each stage.

## Overview

- **Stage 1** -- a shared director base and three kinds (Level, Player, AI); the match preset names the three director
  classes; the GameMode is the hub that creates the Level and Player directors and sends every director the same events in
  order (Level, Players by slot, AI); the GameMode's `On Win Checkpoint Reached` / `On Match Won` delegates are gone. Built;
  43/43 tests pass. Owner committed (5b477b5).
- **Stage 2** -- each side's goals live on its director (`WinConditions` on the Player and AI directors); the rule set no
  longer holds authored goals and copies them from the directors' classes at match start; validation moved with them. Built;
  44/44 tests pass. Owner committed (4bf0cc5).
- **Stage 3** -- lose conditions: `LoseConditions` on the Player and AI directors (any one met = that side loses); wins are
  checked first; a `Lose Condition Met` event then Game Won for the other side; `Opponent Lost` match-end reason; directors
  get `On Lose Condition Met`; the AI's search sees a loss as the end; ignored in a match with no AI. Built; 45/45 tests pass.
  Owner committed (d0c2061).
- **Stage 4** -- level triggers: the Level director's `Triggers` (a condition asset + a side: Player / AI / Either) are checked
  after every committed board change; one that holds fires `On Trigger Fired` once and disarms; `Rearm Trigger` arms it again.
  Not part of win / loss; not seen by the AI's search. Built; 46/46 tests pass. Owner committed (680b16c).
- **Stage 5** -- level changes: the Level director's `Apply Board Operation` (any board operation, made by a faction or nobody,
  through the Mediator without a loadout) and `Set Scoring Rule`, each at a timing (Immediately = right after the move being
  carried out; or Next Turn Start); `EConnectItLoadoutTiming` renamed `EConnectItChangeTiming` (enum redirect). Built; 47/47
  tests pass. Owner committed (bbf8daf).
- **Stage 6** -- vault: recipes for director classes, conditions on directors and lose conditions; new `script-a-level`;
  `ai-turn` updated; design notes' status; the level-trigger question answered; decision note. No code.
