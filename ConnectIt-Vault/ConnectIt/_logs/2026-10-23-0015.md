## Overview

- **The Chained power:** a C++ Sync effect with a native tag. The tile is locked exactly while its partner has no
  piece. When the partner empties, the tile's piece is destroyed first, then the tile locks.
- **The partner link:**
  - a picker on the builder tile, a position in the tile settings and layout cells;
  - on the board, the partner's tile id, which survives shifts;
  - resolved when the board is built and when tiles are added.
- **Seed pass:** the seeded board is settled once under the match's rules (approved by the owner), so chained tiles
  start right.
- **Set Chain Partner** operation and its **Chain Partner Changed** event.
- **Blueprint:** `Get Chained Tiles For`, `Get Chain Partner`.
- **Validation:** builder tiles and layouts.
- **Build and tests:** built; 58/58 tests pass (new `ConnectIt.Board.ChainedTiles`).
- **Throughput not measured:** the CPU was capped at 30% of its maximum frequency the whole time (~68k nodes/s).
