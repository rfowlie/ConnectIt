## Request

Replace No Pieces with a generic Piece Count (drop `StartingPieces` and `bMustHaveScored`), let a side have several win
conditions that must all hold, and make phased ("checkpoint") levels possible by changing win conditions at runtime from a
level Blueprint rather than with engine-side checkpoints.

## Overview

Each side's win conditions are now a group (list) that must all hold; an empty group never wins, and the AI no longer falls
back to the player's. **Piece Count** (Less Than / Equal / Greater Than a count) replaces No Pieces; progress is measured from
the starting piece counts the board now records. The GameMode gained Blueprint-callable `SetWinConditions` / `SetLoadout` and an
`OnWinConditionsMet` event that the Mediator fires before a win is committed, so a level can turn a win into a checkpoint. The
vault explains the flow.
