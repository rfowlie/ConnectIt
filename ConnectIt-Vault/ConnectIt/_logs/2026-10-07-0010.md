## Request

The static `GetLevelConfig` accessor hands out a level's starting data, which goes stale once rules or a player's
actions change mid-match; make the GameMode the keeper of live match data.

## Overview

The GameMode now resolves and owns the live match setup and publishes it: rules + AI profile to a replicated GameState
mirror, loadouts to PlayerStates; clients build their actions from their PlayerState; `GetLevelConfig` is gone (narrow
accessors instead). Build clean; 9/9 tests. Replication needs a 2-player PIE pass. Plan + status:
[design/live-match-setup](../design/live-match-setup.md).
