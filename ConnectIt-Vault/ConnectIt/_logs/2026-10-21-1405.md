## Request

resolving treated as an error: a lock from a change until its visuals complete. Decided:

- ConnectIt-side, not a plugin turn phase.
- The server's own visuals for now.
- Write the plan.

## Overview

Plan written, not approved; no code. Before writing it I read:

- the GameMode's request and level-change paths;
- the Mediator's dispatch;
- the board state component's event enqueue;
- the AI controller's decide / submit loop;
- the event-queue API.

The plan's shape:

- A marker tag queued after each change's events releases a GameMode lock; there is a safety timeout.
- While locked, requests are refused; level changes wait and drain one per release, on the next tick.
- The AI waits for a settled board.
- Stage 2 replicates the flag so clients can block input.

A third collision source turned up while reading: the AI starts its next decision straight after a successful move.
