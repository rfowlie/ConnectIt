## Request

Replace the Per Faction win-condition composite with a win condition parameter for the player and a separate one for the AI on
the level config; the player condition should not default to Score Threshold.

## Overview

`FConnectItRuleSet` now has `PlayerWinCondition` (renamed from `WinCondition`, no default), an optional `AIWinCondition`, and
`AIFaction`, which the GameMode sets after registering the AI. The rule set answers the per-faction questions itself and the
AI's search asks it instead of caching a condition. The Per Faction composite was deleted. Level configs get a validation error
for an empty player condition.
