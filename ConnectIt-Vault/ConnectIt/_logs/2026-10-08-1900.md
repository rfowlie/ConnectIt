## Request

Design the swap-only level (balance input), then add a "No Pieces" win condition and look at different win conditions
for the player and the AI in Adventure mode.

## Overview

Discussed the swap-only economy (swaps conserve pieces, scoring clears four, the AI keeps placing so the board fills) and
split the idea into two levels: a finite army won by clearing it, and a replenished army (3 pieces per score, not built).
Built the win-condition side: `HasFactionWon` on the base condition, a **No Pieces** condition, and a **Per Faction**
composite that assigns a condition to each faction slot. The rule set and the AI's search needed no change.
