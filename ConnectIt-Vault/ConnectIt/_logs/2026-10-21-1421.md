## Overview

- **The lock:** every committed board change locks the GameMode until its completion marker
  (`ConnectIt.Event.BoardChangeComplete`, queued behind its events) completes.
- **While locked:** `ProcessBoardRequest` refuses with a warning. Level changes wait in order and drain one board change
  per release, from the next tick. Turn-start changes join the same line.
- **Timeout:** 15 s by default; it logs what is queued and lifts the lock.
- **The AI:** it decides only from a settled board. It re-decides if the board changed while it thought (a
  committed-change counter on the GameMode).
- **Build and tests:** built; 47/47 tests pass (the level-change test rewritten for the new queue).
- **Not yet checked:** PIE.
