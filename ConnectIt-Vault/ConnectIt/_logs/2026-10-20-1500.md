## Request

After setting up the first presets, the owner's notes led to a discussion (2026-10-20) that settled: MinMax's own time budget
becomes a real default; condition types get neutral "match condition" names; conditions get a context (board + rules) with a
new Not Enough Pieces To Score; per-move legal-move checks with a No Legal Move condition, a Blueprint helper and an AI pass;
and a larger redesign into three directors (Level, Player, AI). Split: part 1 (this plan) builds what the directors don't
affect: [design/conditions-and-legal-moves](../design/conditions-and-legal-moves.md). This note grows with each stage.

- **Stage 0** -- plan moved into the vault.
- **Stage 1** -- MinMax's `TimeBudgetSeconds` -> `DefaultTimeBudgetSeconds`, now used only when the decision gives no budget
  (the director's think time share wins whenever set; before, the tighter of the two). Built; 40/40 tests pass. Owner
  committed (f9fcca4).
- **Stage 2** -- the condition types renamed to neutral "match condition" names, with redirects; no behaviour change. Built;
  40/40 tests pass. Owner committed (0c85ce2).
- **Stage 3** -- conditions are asked with a context (board + the match's rules); scoring rules say how many pieces a score
  needs; new **Not Enough Pieces To Score** condition; conditions can report validation warnings. Built; 41/41 tests pass.
  Owner committed (f869c2d).
- **Stage 4** -- each kind of board move answers "any legal one on this board?" (Place and Swap; the rest Unknown); new **No
  Legal Move** condition; Blueprint helper *Has Any Legal Move (Board State)*; the AI drops moves with no legal instance from
  `Available Request Types` and **passes** when none remain. Built; 42/42 tests pass.
