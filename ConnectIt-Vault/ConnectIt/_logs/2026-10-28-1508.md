## Request

Hover-component plan, Stage 3 (`RefreshFromBoard`); and remove `UGridTrackerSubsystem`, which is unused (owner).

## Overview

- **`IGridBoardActor`** (UnrealGridMechanics): `RefreshFromBoard()`, Blueprint-native with no parameters.
  `AGridTileBase` and `AGridPieceBase` implement it as a no-op.
- **ConnectIt tile:** reads its data from the display board at its position (tile registry) and calls the new
  Blueprint hook `OnRefreshedFromBoard(TileData)`.
- **ConnectIt piece:** takes its position from the piece registry, using the new
  `UGridPieceRegistryBase::FindPositionOfPiece`. Its own `GridPosition` went stale on moves; the refresh now keeps it
  and `OccupiedPosition` in step. It takes its faction from the display board and calls `OnFactionVisualUpdate` on a
  change.
- **Who calls it:** `UConnectIt_BoardStateComponent`, wherever the display board moves on:
  - after each event is applied: the positions whose tile data changed, or every position if the tiles themselves
    changed (layout);
  - on the catch-up to Current;
  - on seed and on a client's first snapshot: every position, after the builder swap.
- `UConnectIt_BoardEventLibrary::RefreshActorsAt(Positions)` does the calls, also from Blueprint. It never creates a
  piece (`GetPieceAtPosition`).
- **`UGridTrackerSubsystem` removed:** nothing read it. `AGridUnitBase` no longer registers with it, and the commented
  call in `AGridTileBase` is gone.
- Editor build clean, 71/71; Game target clean.
