  the chain rule is a settle-step sync rule, so the plan **requires the tile-powers plan** (new `requires:` property,
  added to the convention in `design/__README.md`; tile-powers plan Stage 4 gained the sync-rule hook); chains reuse
  Piece Removed + Tile Lock Changed. **Plan approved by the owner** (properties: `approved: true`, `approved-on:

- [`design/tile-power-setup.md`](../design/tile-power-setup.md) -- owner's direction: a tile's power is its class
