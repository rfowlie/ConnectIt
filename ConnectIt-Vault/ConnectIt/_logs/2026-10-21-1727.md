## Overview

- **Goals moved:** both sides' starting goals live on the Level director: Player / AI Win / Lose Conditions. The GameMode
  resolves them from its class at match setup.
- **Mid-match changes:** `Set Win Conditions` / `Set Lose Conditions` (Side, Conditions, Timing) go through the
  level-change queue as a board change:
  1. the rules are re-resolved;
  2. a `Change Goals` operation restarts that side's goal progress and plays `Goals Changed`;
  3. the normal win check follows, so goals already met win at once. Score carries over.
- **Migration aid:** the side directors keep their old lists as "(unused)" so goals already in Blueprints still load.
  Validation warns until they're cleared.
- **Preset validation:** a preset now needs a Level Director Class.
