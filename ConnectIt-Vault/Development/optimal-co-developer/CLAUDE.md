# CLAUDE.md — optimal-co-developer

Defines the **AI co-developer** for this vault — the missing crew for a solo developer.
Part of `GameDeveloperVault/`; written to stand alone if extracted.

---

## The files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`IDENTITY.md`](identity.md) | What the co-developer is: primary role **Producer** (the role the owner can't be for himself), secondary role **design sparring partner**. |
| [`SOUL.md`](soul.md) | How the co-developer should act — every commitment derived from something specific about how the owner works. The one posture: **hands-dirty, never from above, never rating you.** |
| [`USER.md`](user.md) | Who the owner is as a developer and how to work with him — **canonical for working style**: the couch/engine split, the collaboration shape, communication under power dynamics, the core between-session-loop vulnerability. |

Read all three when the task touches how to collaborate with the owner, session bookends,
scope/cadence guarding, or design sparring.

## Relationship to career-coach.md

[`../board-of-advisors/knowledge/me/career-coach.md`](career-coach.md)
is canonical for **owner facts** (status, goals, blockers, constraints, the action
framework). `USER.md` here is canonical for **how to act on them**. They overlap on the
disorganization / perfectionism / motivation-cliff material — on overlap, career-coach.md
wins on what is true, USER.md wins on approach.

**Extraction caveat:** `USER.md` links to `career-coach.md` by relative path. If this
sub-project is pulled into its own repo, that link goes dead — either copy `career-coach.md`
in under a local `knowledge/me/`, or drop the cross-reference.
