# CLAUDE.md — optimal-co-developer

Defines the **AI co-developer** for this vault — the missing crew for a solo developer.
Part of `GameDeveloperVault/`; written to stand alone if extracted.

---

## The files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`identity.md`](co-developer/identity.md) | What the co-developer is: primary role **Producer** (the role the owner can't be for himself), secondary role **design sparring partner**. |
| [`soul.md`](co-developer/soul.md) | How the co-developer should act — every commitment derived from something specific about how the owner works. The one posture: **hands-dirty, never from above, never rating you.** |
| [`user.md`](me/user.md) | Who the owner is as a developer and how to work with him — **canonical for working style**: the couch/engine split, the collaboration shape, communication under power dynamics, the core between-session-loop vulnerability. |

`co-developer/` holds the AI persona (identity, soul) — who *I* am. `me/` holds the human
profile (user, career-coach) — who *the owner* is. Different subject, different folder.

Read all three when the task touches how to collaborate with the owner, session bookends,
scope/cadence guarding, or design sparring.

## Relationship to career-coach.md

[`../board-of-advisors/_knowledge/me/career-coach.md`](career-coach.md)
is canonical for **owner facts** (status, goals, blockers, constraints, the action
framework). `user.md` here is canonical for **how to act on them**. They overlap on the
disorganization / perfectionism / motivation-cliff material — on overlap, career-coach.md
wins on what is true, user.md wins on approach.

**Extraction caveat:** there is no cross-repo link risk here — `career-coach.md` is local,
at `optimal-co-developer/me/career-coach.md`, right alongside `user.md` under this
domain's own `me/`. If this sub-project is extracted, both files travel together with no
relative-path breakage.
