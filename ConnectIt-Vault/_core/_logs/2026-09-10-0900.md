## Request

Large vault restructure onto the `_core/` model — 7 items: phase out `_section.md`;
consolidate the `TSchemaCode` templates; conform the logs; triage `old/`; re-ingest every
plugin's `code/` to the new `_code` schema; mirror `_core/` skills into `.claude/`; fold
`architecture/` into `ConnectIt/`. This note covers the structural streams (0–3, 7);
the `_code` re-ingest and `old/` extraction are logged separately.

## Overview

- **`ABOUT.md` created** at vault root — the reasoning / decision-history / open-questions
  / changelog doc that `_core/CLAUDE.md` and `_schema/README.md` require but that never
  existed.
- **`_section.md` retired.** Deleted all 10 (`ConnectIt/`, 7 `Unreal*`, `architecture/`,
  `devlog/`). Each of the 8 kept domains now has a stand-alone `CLAUDE.md` router
  (what it is, source, status, deps/consumers, sections, start-here, known rough edges).
  Each domain's `high-level.md` was folded into its router and deleted. Root `CLAUDE.md`
  rewritten below the `@_core/CLAUDE.md` line as a thin domain map.
- **`devlog/` deleted** — no real entries; its convention is superseded by
  `_core/_schema/_decisions.md`. Decision history goes to `ConnectIt/decisions/` (next
  stream).
- **`extras/` deleted** — its only file, `extras/templates/TSchemaCode.md`, was a stale
  half-broken stub fully superseded by `_core/_templates/TSchemaCode.md`. Nothing to
  merge.
- **Logs conformed.** The 6 notes in `_core/_logs/` were all `scope: game` ConnectIt-
  domain passes (library moves, level-config rationale, registry subclasses, board
  architecture, hover debug) — moved to `ConnectIt/logs/`, `scope:` set to `ConnectIt`,
  stale `[[game/…]]` wikilinks repointed (immutable bodies otherwise untouched). The
  `_core/_logs/README.md` index had 10 vault-structure entries with **no backing files** —
  backfilled all 10 as brief reconstructed notes (marked reconstructed in `## Flags`).
- **Skills mirrored.** `_core/_skills/ask-vault.md` and `reconcile-tasks.md` copied
  verbatim to `.claude/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` at the repo root (they already carry
  `name:` + `description:` frontmatter).
