decided should never have existed. Resolved per direction: retire, delete-as-stale, or
re-author-and-mirror as appropriate for each, and remove the mirror-less policy everywhere
it was stated. New `_sessions` schema added since `session-open`/`session-close`'s dated
Open/Close notes didn't fit any existing section shape.
